What this mockup is showing

One help panel, one search box, three views of the same single Crisp knowledge base. There is no second knowledge base, no second Crisp workspace, and no extra subscription. Verified against the Crisp API on August 1, 2026.

  • Read is the knowledge base at help.netnet.app, ranked by Crisp's own search.
  • Watch is the exact same result list, filtered down to the articles that contain a video embed. Net Net detects that structurally by scanning each article's content for Crisp's embed tokens. Nothing has to be tagged by hand.
  • Chat is Crisp's AI agent, named Zimmer here as a placeholder, trained automatically on those same articles, escalating to a real person on the rules in the requirements doc.

Things to click

  1. The question mark in the top bar opens the panel.
  2. Type in the search box, or use the four canned queries in the grey demo bar. Both Watch and Read counts update live.
  3. Try deactivate a contact to see Zimmer refuse to invent a feature that does not exist.
  4. Try cancel my subscription to see a routing rule fire and hand the conversation to a person, with the customer's identity already attached.
  5. Switch persona in the demo bar. Zimmer's answers change, because Crisp is told the role at load time.
  6. Play a video in Watch. Playback happens inside the panel; nobody leaves Net Net.

Sample content is fictional in wording but the article titles, descriptions, and category structure are the real ones live at help.netnet.app today. Requirements for building this are in the companion document.
